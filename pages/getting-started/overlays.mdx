# Overlays

Each overlay has its own settings. Open them with the Settings button on the overlay's [toolbar](/getting-started/layout-mode#overlay-toolbar).

<Screenshot image={overlaySettingsImage} alt="Overlay settings" />

{/* section: Modifiers */}
## Modifiers

{/* setting: Pin */}
### Pin
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Keeps the overlay visible when [Layout Mode](/getting-started/layout-mode) is closed.

{/* setting: AutoHide */}
### Auto Hide
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Hides the overlay when you look away from it, based on the [Overlay Clip Angle](/settings/overlays#overlay-clip-angle), similar to the wrist overlay.

{/* setting: Lock */}
### Lock
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Prevents all interaction with the overlay, including grabbing and clicking.

{/* setting: BlockInput */}
### Block Input
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Prevents mouse input on the overlay.

{/* section: Settings */}
## Settings

{/* setting: TrackingSpace */}
### Tracking Space
<SettingMeta default="World" />

What the overlay is attached to.

- **World**: stays in place in your play space.
- **HMD**: follows your head.
- **Right Controller** / **Left Controller**: follows that controller.

{/* setting: Brightness */}
### Brightness
<SettingMeta default="100%" range="0% – 100%" />

How bright the overlay is.

{/* setting: Opacity */}
### Opacity
<SettingMeta default="100%" range="0% – 100%" />

The overlay's opacity.

{/* setting: MaxFPS */}
### Max FPS
<SettingMeta default="60 FPS" range="1 FPS – 144 FPS" />

The frame rate the overlay updates at while active.

{/* setting: MinFPS */}
### Min FPS
<SettingMeta default="60 FPS" range="1 FPS – 144 FPS" />

The frame rate the overlay updates at while inactive.

{/* setting: CaptureSelect */}
### Capture

The window or monitor this overlay shows. Select it to pick another; the [Windows](/getting-started/layout-mode#overlay-toolbar) button on the overlay's toolbar opens the same list.
