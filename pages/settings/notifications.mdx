# Notifications

<Callout>
Notifications only appear when an external application sends one, or when you unlock an achievement. See [`SendNotification`](/Developer/websocket-api/commands/notifications#sendnotification).
</Callout>

{/* section: Appearance */}
## Appearance

{/* setting: NotificationScale */}
### Notification Scale
<SettingMeta default="0.25" range="0.1 – 2" />

The size of notification popups.

{/* setting: NotificationPositionX */}
### Notification Position X
<SettingMeta default="0" range="-2 – 2" />

Moves notifications left or right relative to your head.

{/* setting: NotificationPositionY */}
### Notification Position Y
<SettingMeta default="-0.125" range="-2 – 2" />

Moves notifications up or down relative to your head.

{/* setting: NotificationPositionZ */}
### Notification Position Z
<SettingMeta default="0.55" range="-2 – 2" />

Moves notifications closer or further away from your head.

{/* setting: SmallNotificationTest */}
### Test Small

Shows a small test notification so you can preview your changes.

{/* setting: LargeNotificationTest */}
### Test Large

Shows a large test notification so you can preview your changes.
