{/* page: wrist */}
# Wrist

{/* section: Wrist_Overlay */}
## Wrist Overlay

{/* setting: AllowWristMovement */}
### Allow Movement
<SettingMeta default="On" />

Lets you grab and reposition the wrist overlay while it's attached to your hand.

{/* setting: HideWristOverlay */}
### Hide Wrist Overlay
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Hides the wrist overlay.

{/* setting: WristOpacity */}
### Wrist Opacity
<SettingMeta default="100%" range="10% – 100%" />

The wrist overlay's opacity.

{/* setting: WristClipAngle */}
### Wrist Clip Angle
<SettingMeta default="60°" range="10° – 180°" />

The angle at which the wrist overlay hides itself. Lower values mean you need to look more directly at it to see it.

{/* setting: ResetWristPosition */}
### Reset Wrist Offsets

Moves the wrist overlay back to its default position on your hand.

{/* section: Date_and_Time */}
## Date / Time

{/* setting: ContinentalTimeFormat */}
### 24 Hour Clock Format
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Shows the clock in 24 hour format, so 1:00 PM becomes 13:00.

{/* setting: DateFormat */}
### Date Format
<SettingMeta default="DD / MM / YY" options="DD / MM / YY, MM / DD / YY, YY / MM / DD" />

The order of day, month and year in the wrist overlay's date.

{/* section: Battery_Widget */}
## Battery Widget

{/* setting: AlwaysShowDetailedInformation */}
### Always Show Details
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Always shows the estimated time remaining for each device. When off, it only shows while you hover over the battery widget.

{/* setting: DefaultShowBatteryPercentage */}
### Show Percentage by Default
<SettingMeta default="On" />

Shows battery levels as percentages.

{/* setting: BatteryFontScale */}
### Battery Font Size
<SettingMeta default="16 px" range="12 px – 24 px" />

The text size of the battery widget.

{/* section: Media_Player */}
## Media Player

{/* setting: AutoMediaDetection */}
### Smart Media Controls
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Opens the wrist media player automatically when media starts playing, and closes it when playback stops.
