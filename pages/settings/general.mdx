# General

<Callout>
Settings are saved to `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Xiexe\XSOverlay\config\overlaySettings.json`.
</Callout>

{/* section: XSOverlay */}
## XSOverlay

{/* setting: AllowAdminPermissions */}
### Allow Admin Permissions
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Lets XSOverlay run with elevated permissions without asking for confirmation on every launch. This is required to interact with elevated windows such as Task Manager. Restart XSOverlay after changing this.

{/* section: Accessibility */}
## Accessibility

{/* setting: DominantHand */}
### Dominant Hand
<SettingMeta default="Right" options="Right, Left" />

Sets your dominant hand. The wrist overlay is placed on the opposite hand, so choosing Left puts it on your right wrist.

{/* setting: Language */}
### Language
<SettingMeta default="English" />

The language used throughout the UI. Translations are provided by the community; language files live in `[XSOverlay install]/XSOverlay_Data/StreamingAssets/localization/`.

{/* setting: Crowdin */}
### Crowdin

Opens the XSOverlay Crowdin project in your browser, where you can contribute or correct translations.

{/* section: Mouse */}
## Mouse

{/* setting: InputMethod */}
### Input Method
<SettingMeta default="Emulate Mouse" />

How pointing at a desktop or window capture is sent to Windows.

- **Emulate Mouse**: moves and clicks the real mouse cursor.
- **Touch Input**: sends Windows touch input, as if you were tapping a touchscreen.

{/* setting: AutomaticMouseControl */}
### Automatic Mouse Control
<SettingMeta default="Off" shownWhen="Input Method is Emulate Mouse" />

Takes control of the mouse cursor as soon as you point at a desktop or window capture. When off, you need to click first to take control.

{/* setting: DoubleClickDelay */}
### Double Click Delay
<SettingMeta default="250 ms" range="0 ms – 1000 ms" shownWhen="Input Method is Emulate Mouse" />

Freezes the cursor in place for this long after a click, making double clicks easier to land.

{/* setting: PointerScale */}
### Pointer Scale
<SettingMeta default="100%" range="25% – 100%" />

The size of the laser pointer cursor shown on overlays.

{/* setting: ScrollSpeed */}
### Scroll Speed
<SettingMeta default="50%" range="0% – 100%" />

How fast the joystick scrolls the content under the pointer.

{/* setting: PointerSmoothing */}
### Pointer Smoothing
<SettingMeta default="100%" range="0% – 100%" />

How much the pointer's movement is smoothed to filter out hand jitter. 0% turns smoothing off.

{/* section: User_Interface */}
## User Interface

{/* setting: HideTooltips */}
### Hide Tooltips
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Hides the tooltips that appear when hovering over buttons.

{/* section: Miscellaneous */}
## Miscellaneous

{/* setting: DiscordRichPresence */}
### Discord Rich Presence
<SettingMeta default="On" />

Shows XSOverlay as your current activity on Discord, alongside the VR game you're running.

{/* setting: LowBatterySound */}
### Low Battery Sound
<SettingMeta default="On" />

Plays a sound when a tracked device's battery drops below the [Low Battery Warning Percent](#low-battery-warning-percent).

{/* setting: LowBatteryWarningPercent */}
### Low Battery Warning Percent
<SettingMeta default="15%" range="5% – 25%" />

The battery level at which a device is considered low.

{/* setting: HapticsStrength */}
### Haptics Strength
<SettingMeta default="100%" range="0% – 100%" />

The strength of haptic feedback, such as when hovering over buttons.

{/* section: Analytics */}
## Analytics

{/* setting: SendAnalytics */}
### Send Analytics
<SettingMeta default="On" />

Sends anonymous usage data that helps improve XSOverlay. It is not tied to you and contains nothing personally identifiable:

- System specs
- HMD model
- Changed settings
- Errors and crash reports
- VR application
- Startup and shutdown
