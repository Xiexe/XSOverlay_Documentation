{/* page: night-light */}
# Night Light

{/* setting: NightLight */}
### Night Light
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Tints your view warmer to reduce blue light.

{/* setting: NightLightIntensity */}
### Intensity
<SettingMeta default="50%" range="0% – 100%" />

How strong the tint is.

{/* section: Schedule */}
## Schedule

{/* setting: NightLightSchedule */}
### Use Schedule
<SettingMeta default="Off" />

Turns Night Light on and off automatically between the start and end hours.

{/* setting: NightLightStartHour */}
### Night Light Start Hour
<SettingMeta default="1" range="1 – 12" shownWhen="Use Schedule is On" />

The hour Night Light turns on.

{/* setting: NightLightStartAMPM */}
### Start AM / PM
<SettingMeta shownWhen="Use Schedule is On" />

Whether the start hour is AM or PM.

{/* setting: NightLightEndHour */}
### Night Light End Hour
<SettingMeta default="1" range="1 – 12" shownWhen="Use Schedule is On" />

The hour Night Light turns off.

{/* setting: NightLightEndAMPM */}
### End AM / PM
<SettingMeta shownWhen="Use Schedule is On" />

Whether the end hour is AM or PM.
